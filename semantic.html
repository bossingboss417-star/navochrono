The Importance of Web Development Skills

By Gonzales, Ace A. | BSIT - Web Track | University of the Cordilleras

Web development skills are essential in today's digital world. Businesses, schools, and organizations rely on websites to share information and deliver services globally.

By learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, students gain the ability to build accessible, well-structured, and interactive webpages. Using semantic HTML elements such as header, nav, main, section, article, aside, and footer also improves webpage readability and search engine optimization.

Quick Highlights

  • Semantic tags give clear meaning to page structure.
  • Web development is a high-demand career path for BSIT graduates.
